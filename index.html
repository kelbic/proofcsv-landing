Questions

Is this tax software or tax advice?

No. ProofCSV is a data formatting and balance reconciliation tool. It computes per-position realized P&L from on-chain data (FIFO within each outcome token) and formats it for import. Tax treatment, rates and jurisdiction rules stay with Koinly and with you or your accountant.

Do you need access to my wallet?

No. Only a public address. We never ask for private keys, seed phrases or exchange credentials.

Why does the file include 2025 transactions?

Balances need the history from the day your wallet was created. Koinly filters the report to 2026 by itself.

Should I also connect the wallet in Koinly?

No. Use the CSV instead of a Polygon wallet sync for the same address, otherwise the history is counted twice.
